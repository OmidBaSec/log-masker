# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a straightforward, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that keeps this project worth contributing to:

* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback — especially on code
  that handles other people's sensitive data, where review is the point.
* Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising to those affected, and
  learning from them.
* Focusing on what is best for the people who use the tool.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

* Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
* Posting real customer log data in an issue, a PR or a test fixture. Use
  synthetic examples.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair
corrective action in response to behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive or harmful — including editing or removing comments, commits, code and
issues, and temporarily or permanently banning a contributor.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through GitHub. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's
privacy and security will be respected.
